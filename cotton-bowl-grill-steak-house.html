About Cotton Bowl Grill & Steak House

Cotton Bowl Grill & Steak House is a casual steakhouse on San Miguel Street in General Santos City, fronting SM Mall and near Metrobank Santiago Branch. Known for its classic steaks and hearty dishes, the restaurant serves grilled meats, tuna, burgers, and other satisfying meals in a relaxed dining setting. It is open daily from 10:30 AM to 9:00 PM, except on Christmas Day, Holy Thursday, Holy Friday, and New Year's Day. For reservations or takeout orders, contact 0921-604-3988 or 554-7455.
