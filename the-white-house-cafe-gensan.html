About The White House Cafè Gensan

The White House Cafè Gensan is a restaurant and coffee shop on Manansala Street in General Santos City. The dining space features warm wood accents, patterned floors, plants, standard tables, and long-table arrangements. Its food selection includes seafood, pizza, prepared meat dishes, fruit, and colorful iced beverages. Visitors may contact the restaurant directly to confirm current opening hours, menu availability, prices, reservations, takeout, delivery, or catering before visiting.
