About Don Carmelo's Smokin' Area

Don Carmelo's Smokin' Area is a barbecue restaurant on Nunez Street in Purok Malakas, Barangay San Isidro, General Santos City. The restaurant serves smokehouse-style grilled meats, steaks, sausages, burritos, calzones, and other hearty dishes in a casual setting with outdoor seating. Frozen bacon is also available for takeout and may also be cooked and served for dine-in. Guests may contact the restaurant directly to confirm current menu availability, prices, reservations, takeout, or event arrangements before visiting.
