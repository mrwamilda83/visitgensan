About The American Backyard Gensan

The American Backyard Gensan is a steakhouse at Champaca corner Camachili Street in Dadiangas East, General Santos City. Its official page highlights steaks, pizzas, pasta, seafood, burgers, and other dishes served in a contemporary dining setting. Guests may contact the restaurant directly to confirm current opening hours, menu availability, prices, reservations, curbside pickup, takeout, or event arrangements before visiting.
