About Sam And Dean

Sam And Dean is a restaurant on Pacifico Bugarin corner De Vera Street in General Santos City. Its menu includes burgers, steaks, tuna, sizzling dishes, nachos, crispy chicken, and other casual meals, served in a relaxed setting with outdoor seating. Guests may contact the restaurant directly to confirm current opening hours, menu availability, prices, reservations, takeout, or event arrangements before visiting.
