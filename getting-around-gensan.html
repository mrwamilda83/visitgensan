Travel Guide

Getting Around GenSan

New to General Santos City? Getting around is easy once you know your options. Here’s your quick guide to local transportation, helpful tips, and the best ways to navigate the city.

Local Transportation

Common Ways to Get Around

Choose the option that works best for your destination, budget, and travel group.

01

Tricycles

Great for short distances or places not accessible by jeepney. Always agree on the fare before your ride starts.

02

Jeepneys

The most common and budget-friendly way to travel around the city. Check the sign board or ask the driver for the destination.

03

Taxis / Grab

Convenient and comfortable, especially with luggage or when traveling with family. Check the Grab app for availability.

04

Private Car / Rental

Ideal if you plan to visit several places in one day or travel outside the city. Car rentals are available in GenSan.

05

Walking

Some areas in the city are walkable, especially around malls, restaurants, and downtown spots. Wear comfortable shoes!

Navigation

Use Google Maps

Keep your mobile data on and use Google Maps to navigate. Save your destination before leaving, especially if your signal might get weak.

Local Help

Ask Locals

Not sure which jeepney or tricycle to take? Show the driver your destination on Google Maps or just ask—locals are friendly and happy to help!

Before You Go

Quick Tips for First-Time Visitors

Keep Cash Ready

Have small bills and coins for jeepneys, tricycles, and small purchases.

Confirm Fares

For tricycles, agree on the fare before riding.

Allow Extra Time

Traffic can get busy during rush hours and peak times.

Stay Connected

Keep your phone charged and mobile data available.

Save Your Hotel

Keep your hotel address saved so you can easily find your way back.

Explore With Confidence

Enjoy the city, explore at your own pace, and make great memories!
